Executive 5-Pillar Retention Playbook

Actionable retention roadmap formulated from empirical behavioral analysis:

1

The Rule of 3 Intervention Protocol

Automate a CRM escalation when a customer reaches 3 service contacts within 12 months to route directly to a Senior Specialist before reaching the 100% churn cliff at 6 contacts.

2

Transaction Velocity Early Alerts

Trigger automated retention incentives whenever a cardholder's quarter-over-quarter transaction ratio drops below 0.70x, counteracting the 50.88% churn risk in advance.

3

Premium Card Value Restructuring

Overhaul rewards and annual fee structures for Platinum (25.0% churn) and Gold (18.1% churn) cardholders by bundling high-end travel perks and flexible spend credits.

4

Product Relationship Bundling

Incentivize single-product customers to link savings, investment, or bill-pay accounts to transition into the 4+ product tier (cutting churn risk by 58%).

5

Zero-Balance Re-Activation

Detect silent card abandonment ($0 revolving balance + 2+ inactive months) and deploy personalized "Spend $100, Get $20" re-activation promos to restore active spend.
